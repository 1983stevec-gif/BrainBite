# BrainBite Art Direction v1.8
Original direction: chunky, friendly, expressive arcade creatures with oversized mouths and simple silhouettes.
Worlds use distinct shapes and motifs:
- Bite Village: cozy hub
- Number Nebula: space/math
- Wordwood: enchanted books/forest
- Language Portals: shifting signage and world objects
Avoid copying MECC/Munchers characters, Troggles, branding, or exact visual presentation.
