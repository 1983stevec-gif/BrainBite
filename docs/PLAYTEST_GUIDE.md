# Family Playtest Guide

Ask the child to play without explaining the controls unless needed.

Observe:
1. Can they start a mission by themselves?
2. Do they understand what to bite?
3. Do they notice the enemy?
4. Do they chase combos?
5. Does a wrong answer feel funny or frustrating?
6. Do they want to replay?
7. Do they care about unlocking a Bite?
8. Which world do they choose voluntarily?
9. Do they understand Memory Drops?
10. Do they ask for more content?

Parent feedback:
- Is the learning summary useful?
- Is Create Practice understandable?
- Is Snap-to-Game review clear?
- Are privacy controls understandable?
- What would make this useful on a school night?
