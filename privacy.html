BrainBite Privacy Policy

BrainBite is designed as a local-first educational game. This page explains the intended data handling model for review.

What We Store

  • Child profile name and gameplay progress.
  • Mastery and review history used to adapt practice.
  • Parent settings, accessibility preferences, and recovery data.
  • Optional cloud-synced family progress when a parent connects Firebase.

What We Do Not Use

  • No open chat or public child profiles.
  • No targeted advertising or behavioral ad profiling.
  • No stranger messaging or public matchmaking.
  • No unnecessary precise location collection.

Parent Controls

  • Parents control profile creation, cloud sync, and recovery actions.
  • Snap-to-Game content must be reviewed before use.
  • Account deletion and export must be verified in the production project before launch.

Launch Status

This policy page is still a draft and requires legal review before public launch. Replace this notice only after the production policy text is approved for the real deployment.
