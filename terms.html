BrainBite Terms of Use

BrainBite is an educational game. These terms describe the intended launch-time rules for review.

Educational Use

  • Mastery values are estimates, not formal assessments.
  • The app is designed for guided practice, review, and family use.
  • Parents remain responsible for reviewing child-facing content and cloud features.

Accounts and Data

  • Child progress is stored locally by default.
  • Cloud sync is optional, parent-controlled, and limited to a minimized progress record.
  • Worksheet images and text, device-local parent security data, and play-time ledgers are not synchronized.
  • Parent-requested recovery exports may include local legacy records retained for recovery.
  • Account deletion and export behavior must be verified in production before launch.

Acceptable Use

  • Do not attempt to bypass parent controls or recover another family's data.
  • Do not rely on the app as a substitute for formal educational evaluation.
  • Use the app on supported devices and keep browser/app updates current where practical.

Launch Status

This terms page is still a draft and requires legal review before public launch. Replace this notice only after the production terms text is approved for the real deployment.
